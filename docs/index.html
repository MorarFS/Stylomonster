From Word-Length Histograms to Hypersphere Verification

  • 1887 T. C. Mendenhall proposes Characteristic Curves of Composition (word-length distributions) to test Shakespeare vs. Bacon.
  • 1944 G. Udny Yule introduces Yule's K Characteristic to measure vocabulary repetition independent of sample size.
  • 1964 Mosteller & Wallace resolve the 12 disputed Federalist Papers using Bayesian/discriminant rates of 30 function words (upon, whilst).
  • 2002 John F. Burrows introduces Delta (Δ_B)—mean absolute distance over corpus-standardized z-scores of the Most Frequent Words.
  • 2014 Koppel & Winter introduce the General Imposters (GI) Monte Carlo framework for open-set authorship verification.
  • 2017 Evert et al. (Würzburg) prove that L2-normalized Cosine Delta (Δ_cos) outperforms Manhattan Delta across languages and MFW sizes.
